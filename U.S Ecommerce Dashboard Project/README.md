# 📊 U.S. E-Commerce Performance Dashboard – Power BI

**An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing U.S. e-commerce sales and profit performance** across multiple dimensions including product categories, customer segments, shipping methods, and geographical regions.

This dashboard provides comprehensive insights into **sales trends, profitability analysis, and operational efficiency** for data-driven business decisions.

## 🚀 Features

### 🎛️ Interactive Navigation
- **Dual Dashboard System**: Seamlessly switch between Sales and Profit dashboards
- **Cross-Filtering**: Selections in one dashboard affect visualizations in both
- **Drill-Through Capabilities**: Navigate from summary to detailed views

### 📊 Dynamic Filters & Slicers
- **Time Period Selection**: Monthly, quarterly, yearly analysis
- **Geographical Filtering**: State-level, regional, or national views
- **Product Category Selection**: Office Supplies, Furniture, Technology
- **Customer Segment Filtering**: Corporate, Consumer, Home Office
- **Shipping Mode Analysis**: Same Day, First Class, Second Class, Standard Class

## 🖱️ How to Use the Dashboard

### Navigation Instructions
1. **Open Power BI Desktop** and load the .pbix file
2. **Use the navigation buttons** at the top to switch between Sales and Profit dashboards
3. **Apply filters** using the sidebar slicers for customized views
4. **Hover over visuals** to see detailed tooltips
5. **Click on chart elements** to apply cross-filtering
6. **Use drill-through** for state or product-level details
7. **Export data** from any visual for further analysis

### Key Interactions
- **Click a state** to see all related transactions
- **Select a month** to analyze seasonal patterns
- **Filter by category** to compare product performance
- **Adjust date ranges** for trend analysis

## 🔍 Business Insights & Recommendations

### Strategic Findings
1. **Product Category Performance**
   - Technology products contribute 30.08% of sales but need profitability analysis
   - Furniture shows moderate performance with room for growth

2. **Customer Segment Analysis**
   - Consumer segment dominates (52.01%) but may have lower margins
   - Corporate segment shows balanced sales and potential for B2B expansion

3. **Geographical Performance**
   - California leads in sales, indicating strong market presence
   - Opportunity to expand in underperforming states

4. **Shipping Efficiency**
   - Standard Class shipping dominates (59.87%) but may impact customer satisfaction
   - Consider optimizing faster shipping options for premium segments

5. **Seasonal Trends**
   - December shows peak profitability (18.94% of annual profit)
   - Consider targeted marketing campaigns during high-performing months

### Actionable Recommendations
1. **Optimize Product Mix**: Focus on high-margin technology products
2. **Segment-Specific Strategies**: Develop targeted approaches for each customer segment
3. **Geographical Expansion**: Invest in marketing for underperforming states
4. **Shipping Strategy**: Balance cost and customer satisfaction in shipping options
5. **Seasonal Planning**: Align inventory and marketing with profit peaks
